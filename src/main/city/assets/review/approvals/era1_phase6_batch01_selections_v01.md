## Evolution Lock

The civilization must not replace one complete era painting with another. Earlier terrain, buildings, routes, and historical traces persist at stable world coordinates. Growth is cumulative and staged:

```text
existing world
→ connection and terrain reveal
→ routes and circulation extend
→ ecology establishes
→ civic forms accumulate
→ local light and fine detail settle
```

Continuous committed readings control density, maturity, illumination, circulation, and activity within an era. An era boundary authorizes new spatial and visual possibilities; it does not instantly redecorate the complete world. Whole Civilization View may refit around newly revealed bounds, but ordinary exploration remains focused and never exposes a world-sized rectangle as geography.
