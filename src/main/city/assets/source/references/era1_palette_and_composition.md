## Composition Lock

- Canvas 1920 × 1200, fixed elevated three-quarter camera.
- Central hearth at normalized `(0.50, 0.66)`.
- Civic focus remains within x 25–75%, y 25–80%.
- Cropping may remove distant atmosphere and the lowest 12% foreground, never the hearth or required route anchors.
- Liquid crosses the lower-middle plane; routes connect hearth to west/east shelters and continue beyond frame.
- Utility UI is external code-native glass and is never painted into a source asset.

## State Review Composites

- Newborn: low local light, still current, latent rather than empty.
- Active: localized amber hearth, restrained teal current, visible routes and civic silhouettes.
- Dimmed: reduced masks and motion; permanent geometry unchanged.
- Hibernating: zero local metabolic emission; starlight silhouette separation only.
- Returning: teal route arrival followed by amber recovery; no full-screen celebration.
