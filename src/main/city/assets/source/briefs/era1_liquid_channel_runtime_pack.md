## Runtime Contract

Each base is a 512 × 512 straight-alpha PNG with isolated irregular mineral-root banks and dark non-emissive liquid. The water reads as almost-black blue rather than empty transparency. Source art contains no animated current, focus pulse, foam, particles, bloom, or cast light; those remain renderer-controlled.

- Channel V01: left entry `(0.00, 0.52)`, right exit `(1.00, 0.48)`, one calm broad bend.
- Channel V02: left entry `(0.00, 0.48)`, right exit `(1.00, 0.52)`, a shallow compatible S variation.
- Pool V01: connected left and right mouths at the same widths, widening into one asymmetrical low pool without reading as a lake plate.

Banks are worn mineral-root lips, not regular masonry. Embedded roots remain sparse and may cross beneath the surface, never forming a bridge or the woven grammar of the root-path kit.

## Shared Negative Contract

Exclude terrain swatches, rectangular ground slabs, canal curbs, roads, trenches, pipes, bridges, buildings, citizens, fish, boats, waterfalls, rapids, foam, waves, bright reflections, glow, neon, magic, lava, acid, slime, steam, smoke, particles, text, symbols, labels, UI, signatures, and watermarks.

## Acceptance

All declared mouths touch the canvas edges at compatible widths. The liquid remains visible with animation disabled and during hibernation. Bases align beneath the independent code-native current response at Detail, District, and Whole Civilization scales.
