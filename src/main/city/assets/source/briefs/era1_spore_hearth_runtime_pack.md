It must remain lower and wider than the ordinary shelters, approachable from the near edge, and unmistakably public. Its stable illustrated body and state-controlled internal light are separate paired runtime assets.

Preserve the shallow radial basin, protected central spore-organ cluster, layered public gathering rim, broad rooted contact, and inward-curling prong rhythm.
