## Purpose And Registration

Three independent transparent ecology clusters add layered habitat, occlusion, and close-detail passages to the Era I basin. They are living environmental growths, never dwellings or civic structures.

- Runtime export: 512 × 512 straight-alpha PNG per variant.
- Camera: approved elevated three-quarter view.
- Anchor: normalized `(0.50, 0.94)` at central root contact.
- Crop-safe bounds: left `0.03`, top `0.03`, right `0.97`, bottom `0.97`.
- Depth use: one background-capable tall cluster, one midground spreading cluster, one smaller near/juvenile cluster.
- Semantic LODs: Detail, District, Overview through renderer scaling.

## Visual Contract

Preserve braided central growth, multiple overlapping shelf levels, asymmetrical juvenile clusters, strong root contact, and open negative spaces between trunks. Widen selected gaps beneath the shelves for visual travel, break repeated shelf shapes, and prevent a perfectly tiered architectural profile. There are no enclosed interiors, doors, rooms, balconies, constructed platforms, paths, terrain, light, fog, particles, or citizens.

## Negative Contract

No mushroom house, cottage, hut, shelter, civic or specialist building, tower, temple, altar, throne, stairs, bridge, door, window, face, furniture, lantern, flame, smoke, glow, machinery, text, logo, signature, or watermark.

## Acceptance

- Chroma-key removal, despill, edge contraction, straight-alpha cleanup, and zero transparent RGB pass.
- Every root and shelf edge is complete and the common anchor remains stable.
- At least two open trunk gaps remain visible at Detail scale.
- Canopy silhouettes remain visually different from shelter and spore-hearth architecture.
- Three variants are registered, hashed, validated, and reviewed in travel, Detail, District, and Whole Civilization views.
