# Human Normalization Checklist

Selected tools: Krita or Photoshop for layered paint/alpha work; Aseprite for sprite registration and frame cleanup. Generative fill is optional and must receive its own provenance entry.

For every selected output:

1. Reconcile perspective and horizon to the locked reference.
2. Remove pseudo-text, signatures, symbols, unintended faces, and repeated model artifacts.
3. Paint missing edges; separate baked citizens, particles, atmosphere, and light.
4. Re-grade to the approved palette without crushing dark separation.
5. Author masks from cleaned bases at exact matching dimensions and origin.
6. Inspect straight alpha on black, white, and magenta checkerboards; repair RGB under transparency.
7. Verify anchors, crop bounds, and ground contact.
8. Export the declared format and dimensions, then calculate SHA-256.
9. Complete provenance and license records.
10. Review newborn, active, dimmed, hibernating, and returning runtime composites.

No model output moves directly from generation into `runtime/`.
