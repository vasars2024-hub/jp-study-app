# Noctis Asset Package

This directory is the Phase 5 handoff boundary and the active Phase 6 controlled-production package. Era I environment references are approved, and runtime production is proceeding family by family.

- `manifests/asset-manifest.json` contains accepted runtime files. Ordinary shelters, the paired ancestral spore-hearth, three fungal-canopy ecology variants, and the four-role root-path kit are registered; remaining families continue to use code-native fallbacks until their gates pass.
- `manifests/asset-inventory.json` is the complete requested Era I region, seam, strata-preview, and semantic-LOD inventory.
- `manifests/scene-manifest.json` locks composition, layers, world coordinates, connected regions, semantic LODs, focus targets, and code-native fallbacks.
- `manifests/provenance.json` is the provenance and license ledger template.
- `source/briefs/` and `source/prompts/` are the approved generation instructions.
- `source/references/` locks the palette and composition reference.
- `source/cleanup/` specifies human normalization.
- `review/` holds contact sheets and signed review records.

Generated outputs first enter controlled source or review storage, never directly into `runtime/`. Promotion requires locked-reference compliance, technical normalization, provenance, approval, a content hash, in-scene review, and a passing `validateAssetPackage()` result.

The current scene retains a complete navigable code-native fallback beneath incremental production assets. Missing art reduces fidelity without changing simulation truth, camera behavior, or the shape of revealed space. Approved contact sheets remain references, not full-world backgrounds or direct runtime exports.
