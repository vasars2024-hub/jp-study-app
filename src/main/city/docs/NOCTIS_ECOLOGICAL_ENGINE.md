# Noctis Ecological Engine

## Purpose

This document is the architectural blueprint and core validation engine for the biological, sensory, and technological systems of Noctis.

It defines how real-world abyssal, deep-sea, troglobitic, and cave-dwelling evolutionary adaptations govern the civilization's mechanics, fauna, architecture, and technology.

These references are biological and architectural analogs. Noctis is not a literal underwater game, cave simulator, or full 3D exploration space. The analogs constrain how a dark-adapted 2D/2.5D civilization behaves inside the Study OS.

**Boundary rule:** Constraints in this document apply to diegetic in-world assets, citizen biology, and simulation overlays. The human-facing Study OS interface (calendar, notes, dashboard, readable labels, Fluent UI) is governed by `VISION.md` and `ART_DIRECTION.md` and must remain practical and legible.

**Terminology lock:** *Spore-hearth* means cold bioluminescent civic light without combustion, smoke, or industrial heat. *Warm-colored glow* in upper-tier docs refers to human-facing visual hue only, never thermal warmth.

This document acts as an automated constraint engine for any future AI-generated features, districts, tools, or units.

## 1. The Deep-Dark Biological Blueprint: The Noctae

The citizens, the Noctae, are biologically non-human. They use real-world evolutionary traits found in high-pressure abyssopelagic zones and absolute-darkness cave networks while remaining emotionally legible as a civilization.

### Visual System: Abyssopelagic Mechanics

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|---------------|
| Anatomy | Modeled after Anomalops katoptron, splitfin flashlight fish, and deep-sea teleosts. Oversized crystalline lenses pair with a highly reflective, multi-layered guanine tapetum lucidum. |
| Perception | Cannot perceive a standard daylight color spectrum. Vision is optimized for high-contrast grayscale and deep ultraviolet wave-bands, capturing faint starlight in eternal night. |
| Mechanical interaction | Standard ambient illumination blinds them. The world is perceived through contrasting fields of soft cold emissions and absolute dark silhouettes. |

### Non-Visual Sensory Apparatus: Troglobitic Mechanics

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|---------------|
| Anatomy | Modeled after cave-dwelling Astyanax mexicanus, blind cavefish. Highly developed cranial lateral-line systems form rows of microscopic, fluid-filled sensory pits along jawlines, temples, and collarbones. |
| Perception | Detects minute barometric fluctuations, micro-acoustic vibrations, and low-frequency airflow patterns. |
| Mechanical interaction | Can feel air displacement from sliding doors, structural collapse, or passing citizens long before visual contact. |

### Bioluminescent Photophores And Metabolic Signaling

| Aspect | Specification |
|--------|---------------|
| Anatomy | Subcutaneous photophore organs run along limbs and sensory spires. Symbiotic chemiluminescent bacteria are modeled after deep-sea anglerfish and Lampyridae beetle light chemistry. |
| Communication | No loud vocalizations in civic spaces. Muscular skin folds act as mechanical shutters to flash, douse, or pulse photophores as bioluminescent semaphores. |
| State signaling | Sharp rapid teal pulse means active cognitive focus. Slow undulating low-intensity amber wave means offline or resting state. |

## 3. Esoteric Tech Evolution Across Eras

Because the Noctae possess non-visual and tactile sensory profiles, in-world Noctae technology cannot use flat screens, printed text, or high-heat energy grids. This constraint applies to diegetic tools, buildings, and simulation overlays, not to the human-facing Study OS interface, which must remain readable and useful.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         ESOTERIC TECH EVOLUTION                             |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| ERA 1: SPORE-HEARTH        -> Tactile Quipu Knots & Echolocation Resonance  |
| ERA 2: CRYSTAL INSCRIPTION -> Luciferin Aqueducts & Internal Light Pipes    |
| ERA 3: BIO-CIRCUITRY       -> Optogenetic Mycelial Computers & Aurora Beams |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Era 1: The Spore-Hearth Era

- Data storage: Raised tactile fungal-knot cords, using an Andean Quipu analog, read by highly sensitive fingertip nerve endings.
- Mapping infrastructure: Trained echolocation fauna. Return pings are captured on acoustic tympanic resonators, hollow resonant mushroom caps that vibrate at specific frequencies.
- Civic light: Cold spore-hearths emit bioluminescent glow without combustion, smoke, or industrial heat.

### Era 2: The Crystal And Inscription Era

- Power and illumination grid: Cold chemiluminescent liquid is piped through glass plumbing networks synthesizing luciferin and luciferase biochemically.
- Light routing: Internally reflecting fiber-optic crystal light-pipes bend and funnel cold light onto work surfaces without industrial heat or glare.
- Record keeping: Tactile inscriptions, raised fiber scrolls, and crystal memory lattices replace paper books.

### Era 3: The Bio-Circuitry And Alchemical Era

- Computing architecture: Living neural-mycelial computers.
- Optogenetic input: Real-time study inputs act as optogenetic triggers. High-frequency cold light flashes stimulate organic circuits to grow, calculate, and route data through biomorphic glass fiber paths along streets.

## 5. Metabolic Transmutation Loops: Core Simulation Loop

The transition from real-world cognitive effort to simulated ecological growth is governed by strict metabolic pathways. AI must use this schema to translate incoming session metrics into environmental mutations.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         METABOLIC PATHWAY SCHEMA                            |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Cognitive Input       -> Primary Nutrient        -> Ecological Output        |
| Mathematics/Logic     -> Piezo-Electric Brine    -> Crystal Lattice Growth   |
| History/Languages     -> Chemosynthetic Glucans  -> Mycelial Substrate Nodes |
| Creative/Arts         -> Luciferin Catalysts     -> Photophore Mutation      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Mathematics And Logic Pathway

- Nutrient profile: Hyper-dense ionized mineral pools, called piezo-electric brine.
- Ecological output: Brine seeps into crystalline mountain plates, forcing rapid geometric crystal lattice expansion.
- Systemic benefit: Local power grids reduce metabolic decay rate of surrounding districts during user offline periods.

### History, Law, And Language Pathway

- Nutrient profile: Deep carbon-rich chemosynthetic glucans.
- Ecological output: Triggers rapid cellular division in subterranean mycelial root mats beneath city floors.
- Systemic benefit: Expanding root networks breach buried shale vaults, unearthing historical data shards, rare decorative spores, and hidden blueprint schemas.

### Creative Arts And Linguistics Pathway

- Nutrient profile: Concentrated luciferin catalysts.
- Ecological output: Alters biological pigment of local fauna and citizens' photophore arrays.
- Systemic benefit: Unlocks complex flashing semaphore dialects among the Noctae, boosts structural efficiency, shifts architectural themes, and triggers atmospheric aurora curtains across the background skyline.

## 8. Document Validation Protocol

Critical execution rule:

This engine document is the domain authority on environmental physics, citizen biology, and technological paradigms for the Noctis module after the concept has passed `VISION.md` and `ART_DIRECTION.md`.

Any generated in-world content that introduces standard combustion fire, daylight, high-heat metallurgy, vocal speech pattern mechanics, or traditional human eyesight anatomy is inherently invalid and must be rejected and rewritten to maintain the integrity of the Abyssal/Troglobitic Constraint Engine.

### AI Rejection Checklist

Reject and rewrite if the in-world feature includes any of:

- [ ] Standard combustion fire, forges, smoke, or industrial heat
- [ ] Daylight, sun cycles, or blinding ambient glare
- [ ] Diegetic paper books, Noctae-facing flat screens, or daylight-readable in-world text UI
- [ ] Vocal speech as primary citizen communication
- [ ] Human daylight-adapted eyesight anatomy
- [ ] Isolated structures not anchored to mycelial or crystal networks
