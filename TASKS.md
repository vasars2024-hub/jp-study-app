  - **De-inflection engine — `src/shared/deinflect.ts`** (pure, framework-free, cross-process — same home/pattern as `sentenceBounds.ts`/`mining.ts`). Yomichan-style iterative rule engine: each rule rewrites a kana *suffix* (`kanaIn`→`kanaOut`) gated by grammatical word-type flags (v1/v5/vs/vk/vz/adj-i as a bitfield); a BFS peels one suffix per step from the surface form, so the first time a candidate root is reached it's via the shortest conjugation chain. Godan families are generated from あ/い/え/お-row + 音便 (past/te) column tables rather than hand-typed (far less transcription error). Covers: polite (ます and its past/negative/volitional/te inflections), negative, past, te, causative (させる/せる + short さす/す), passive, potential (incl. する→できる, ら抜き), volitional, imperative, both conditionals (ば/たら), たり, たい, すぎる, adverbial (く), progressive (ている/てる), completion (てしまう + ちゃう/じゃう contractions), preparatory (ておく/とく), plus explicit する/来る kana-form irregulars and adjective forms. Composition is automatic: 食べさせられた peels past→passive→causative to 食べる and the chain is flipped to inner→outer for display (`['causative','passive/potential','past']`). **Returns ALL candidates ranked, not the first hit** (the plan's explicit pitfall): kana いった yields both いく (行く, irregular 音便) and いう (regular った→う); the dictionary is the tiebreak. Depth-capped at 10 with a `term|flags` visited-set so it always terminates. 21 unit tests in `src/shared/__tests__/deinflect.test.ts` cover every verb class, adjectives, the stacked causative-passive-past chain, contractions, progressive, the いった ambiguity, ranking (shorter chains first), and termination on empty/non-JP input.
