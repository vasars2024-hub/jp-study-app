one strong containment cue"*. Full census: `CENSUS_BOXES.md`.

| # | Finding |
|---|---|
| B1 | **46 container families paint two or more strong cues** — 11 paint all three. Four are documented exemptions at `styles.css:23336` |
| B2 | **`.ui-card`, the canonical card primitive, is itself a violation and has zero consumers.** The guarded rule drops the border and leaves `box-shadow` standing → fill + shadow. Its header comment describes two cues in the sentence citing the rule permitting one. Verified: the class appears exactly once in all TSX (`Surfaces.tsx:14`) |
| B3 | **15 of 30 shared primitives have no consumer outside `components/ui/`** — a design system nothing adopted |
| B4 | **`--mc-*` is a second design system.** `mediaCenter.css` holds **134 hex / 429 rgba** literals; `--mc-accent: #d84a68` is not the app's `--accent` |
